"Teaching was my passion. KnowQuest is my purpose."

While working with educational organizations and international students during my Computer Science studies, I noticed a critical gap facing modern learners: many struggle with English communication confidence and lack early exposure to practical tech skills.

I founded KnowQuest to bridge this gap — creating an inclusive global platform that unites students from all around the world under personalized mentorship.

Our Dual Missions:

  • ✦ Communication & Tech Readiness: Equipping students with Spoken English fluency alongside high-demand CS skills in AI, Web Development, and Data Science for international academic and career success.
  • ✦ Empowering Young Creators: Guiding young minds from Scratch to AI — transforming kids from passive game players into creative logical thinkers and problem solvers.
